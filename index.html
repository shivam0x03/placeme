Data notes & sources

What this dashboard can support, and where its limits matter.

01

Institution-level workbook

The supplied spreadsheet lists colleges and published placement summaries. It does not contain anonymised student-level records, assessments or verified skills.

02

Reported figures stay intact

Placement notes and package fields keep the original wording. Offers are not converted into placement rates, and unlike cohorts are not combined.

03

Learning plan is advisory

Role skill lists follow the supplied project synopsis. Resource picks are learning suggestions, not employer benchmarks or guaranteed placement outcomes.
